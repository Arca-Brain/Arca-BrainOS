# Process-Driven AI & CMA Method with Eliott Meunier

## ⚡ Executive Summary
Enterprise AI implementations fail when approached piecemeal rather than Process-Driven. To capture real time savings, tacit knowledge must be explicated through the CMA Method (Clarify, Map, Amplify).

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- AI failure stems from lack of system [[03:27](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uZAIEFBOc4&t=207)].
- The 3-step CMA method: Clarify, Map, Amplify [[04:10](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uZAIEFBOc4&t=250)].
- Prioritization by frequency: Trigger classification [[05:26](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uZAIEFBOc4&t=326)].
- Supervised Agent vs. Pure Automation decision tree [[11:50](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uZAIEFBOc4&t=710)].
