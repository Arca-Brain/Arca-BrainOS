# 📝 Stop Prompting Claude: The Karpathy Method to Multiply Your Productivity

## ⚡ Executive Summary
This video by Austin Marchese proposes moving beyond simple traditional prompting to adopt a systems engineering framework inspired by Andrej Karpathy. Through a three-layer framework: Specification (Spec), Scratchpad (persistent working memory), and Feedback Loop, users structure their interactions with Claude as a software collaboration.
