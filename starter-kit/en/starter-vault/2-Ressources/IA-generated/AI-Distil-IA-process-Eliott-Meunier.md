# 🧪 AI Distillation: Process-Driven AI (CMA Method & Process Cards)

## ⚡ Executive Summary
Ad-hoc conversational AI usage fails to deliver lasting productivity gains. To capture real ROI, organizations must adopt a Process-Driven approach by explicating tacit knowledge using the CMA Method (Clarify, Map, Amplify). By mapping daily workflows according to their triggers (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, On Trigger, Manual) and writing standardized process cards (Inputs, Steps, Method, Outputs), AI integration becomes structured: either as supervised agents for judgment-heavy tasks or as background automations (n8n) for deterministic repetitive tasks.

## 🔑 Core Concepts

- **Key Takeaways:**
  - 80% of AI integration projects fail due to lack of structure and un-documented processes.
  - Real time gains come from systematic, repeatable process engineering rather than ad-hoc chat prompting.
  - Method is more important than model choice: output quality depends directly on the excellence embedded in the process card.

- **Conceptual Models:**
  - **CMA Method:** Clarify (context & identity), Map (workflows & AI intervention points), Amplify (agents & automations).
  - **Trigger Matrix:** Workflow classification by trigger frequency to assess automation ROI.
  - **Agent vs. Automation Decision Tree:** Agents assist humans in judgment tasks; Automations run headless for deterministic steps.
