# 🧪 AI Distillation: The Karpathy Method for Claude (Spec, Scratchpad, Feedback)

## ⚡ Executive Summary
Austin Marchese proposes rejecting throwaway conversational prompting in favor of a 3-layer systems engineering framework inspired by Andrej Karpathy: co-designing an agile Specification (Spec), using a persistent Scratchpad to maintain context without overloading window limits, and establishing a Feedback Loop where corrections amend a permanent "LLM Wiki" (`AGENTS.md`).

## 🔑 Core Concepts

- **Key Takeaways:**
  - Understanding cannot be outsourced: human intellectual design stays paramount while AI handles execution.
  - Traditional prompting fails on complex tasks because it forces AI to guess implicit requirements instead of relying on a structured spec.
  - Isolated chat corrections are ineffective; every correction must update the project's rules engine to prevent error recurrence.

- **Conceptual Models:**
  - **3-Layer Karpathy Framework:**
    - *Layer 1: Specification (Spec)*: Task definition broken into agile blocks with explicit acceptance criteria.
    - *Layer 2: Scratchpad (Working Memory)*: Persistent file tracking action plans, hypotheses, and self-audits.
    - *Layer 3: Feedback Loop & Environment*: Automated test suites and LLM Wiki (`AGENTS.md`).
