# ✍️ Note: AI Context & Intentional Friction

Most AI workflows fail because they attempt to eliminate 100% of human friction. But not all friction is bad.

## The Distinction Between Two Types of Friction

1. **Administrative Friction (Eliminated by AI):**
   YAML metadata formatting, file relocation across directories, wikilink suggestions (`[[...]]`), and worklog entry logging.
2. **Intentional Friction (Preserved for Humans):**
   Cognitive session framing, strategic priority choices, initial idea drafting, and final decision-making.

By delegating administrative friction to AI agents via specialized skills (`arca-distill`, `arca-inbox-process`), humans retain 100% of their energy for intentional friction and Deep Work.
